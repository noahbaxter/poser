# Poser — Preset List

## Mics (~18-20)

### Kick / Bass Drum

| Mic | Key Character | Why Essential |
|-----|--------------|---------------|
| AKG D112 | Huge low-end hump ~80-100Hz, clicky presence peak at 4kHz | Super distinctive, the "kick drum mic" |
| Shure Beta 52A | Tighter low end than D112, less hyped click | Modern kick standard |
| Electro-Voice RE20 | Flatter than expected, "broadcast smooth" | Crosses categories — kick, vocal, broadcast |
| Sennheiser e602 | Scooped mids, very present | Modern metal kick sound |

### Snare / Guitar Cabs

| Mic | Key Character | Why Essential |
|-----|--------------|---------------|
| Shure SM57 | 5-6kHz presence peak, tight low-end rolloff | THE sound of rock snare and guitar cab |
| Sennheiser MD421 | Mid scoop ~400Hz, fatter and wider than 57 | Toms, guitar cabs, the "broadcast" dynamic |
| Sennheiser e906 | Similar to 421 but more presence, less low-mid | Guitar cab staple, quick flat-profile swap |
| Beyerdynamic M88 | Tight, controlled, less hyped | The "anti-hype" dynamic, great on kick too |

### Vocals / General Purpose

| Mic | Key Character | Why Essential |
|-----|--------------|---------------|
| Shure SM7B | Smooth, controlled, broad presence shelf | THE podcast/hip-hop vocal sound |
| Electro-Voice RE20 | (duplicate — same curve as kick category) | Crosses kick/vocal/broadcast |
| Shure SM58 | Brighter and more nasal than SM7B | Live vocal sound, presence-forward |

### Condensers

| Mic | Key Character | Why Essential |
|-----|--------------|---------------|
| Neumann U87 | Broad, slightly hyped presence | The "expensive vocal" curve |
| AKG C414 | Flatter, more clinical, different presence peak shape | Studio workhorse, multi-pattern reference |
| Neumann KM184 | Crisp, detailed high end | Small diaphragm overhead sound |
| AKG C451 | Very distinctive high-end character | Classic overhead / acoustic guitar |

### Ribbons

| Mic | Key Character | Why Essential |
|-----|--------------|---------------|
| Royer R-121 | Gentle high-end rolloff | Guitar cab favorite, the "smooth" option |
| Coles 4038 | Different rolloff shape, warmer | BBC broadcast character |
| AEA R84 | Classic ribbon warmth, fuller low end | Vintage ribbon sound |

**Note:** Condenser and ribbon curves won't capture transient behavior or capsule resonance — just the frequency response. That's still the single biggest factor in their identity and worth including.

## Cabs (~10-12)

### Speakers (the tonal identity)

| Speaker | Character |
|---------|-----------|
| Celestion Vintage 30 | Modern rock/metal, presence peak ~3.5kHz, slightly scooped |
| Celestion Greenback | Warmer, more midrange, classic rock |
| Celestion Blue | Chimey, bright, Vox territory |
| Jensen P12R / C12N | Fender cleans, scooped and bell-like |
| Eminence Texas Heat (or equiv) | Fender-voiced |
| Celestion Creamback | Halfway between V30 and Greenback |

### Cab Configurations

| Config | Character |
|--------|-----------|
| 4x12 closed back + V30s | Mesa/Marshall modern |
| 4x12 closed back + Greenbacks | Classic Marshall |
| 2x12 open back | Fender Twin territory |
| 1x12 open back | Deluxe Reverb |
| 1x12 closed back | Smaller modern amp |

**Note:** Whether speakers and cabs ship as separate selectable components (modular) or as combined whole-chain presets depends on the decomposition validation results.
